# Battle pass references (2026-10-26)

Why: the owner wants the Passe de Batalha to sell the pass through gamification, the way LoL, Valorant and CoD passes do. This file records the patterns those passes use and maps each one to facts the panel already holds (`docs/screen-inventory.md` §8). A pattern that would need a new fact is listed as a question, not built.

## What the reference passes do

| Pattern                             | Who does it                                                   | Mechanism                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Two lanes, free and paid, per level | LoL, Valorant, Destiny 2, EA FC Premium Pass, Finch (Mobbin)  | The empty free slot beside a paid reward shows what the player is missing. Single merged tracks with a "free" tag (Halo, CoD) confuse players about why a reward is locked. |
| Headline reward as the hero         | LoL (prestige skin), Valorant (tier 50 skin), Rocket League   | The last reward of the season is shown big, from day one, on the free path too.                                                                                             |
| Chapters with peaks                 | Valorant (10 chapters × 5 tiers), Fortnite (pages)            | Rewards grouped in tens, with highs and lows, so progress has milestones instead of an endless strip.                                                                       |
| Buy later, keep what you earned     | Valorant, Rocket League                                       | Buying the pass mid-season grants the paid rewards for every tier already reached. It turns time already played into a reason to buy.                                       |
| Tier skip on purchase               | Rocket League bundles, RBG itself ("dá 5 níveis na hora")     | The purchase shows the player jumping ahead, with the rewards they land on.                                                                                                 |
| Missions feed one progress bar      | EA FC (Season Points), Valorant (contracts and pass together) | Each mission shows what it adds to the next level, so daily play reads as progress on the pass.                                                                             |
| Real deadline                       | All of them                                                   | The season end is always visible. Ethical floor: only the true end date, never a fake timer.                                                                                |
| Value at the purchase point         | Fortnite (total-value framing, Game Developer)                | The buy block counts what the pass unlocks against its price.                                                                                                               |

## Direction

Gamification here comes from structure and art, not from the gamer-UI defaults the rules ban: the two lanes, the headline reward big, the jump the purchase gives, and missions that count down to the next level. Glow, gold, rarity frames and fake timers stay out (`rbg-taste`, `hallmark`).
