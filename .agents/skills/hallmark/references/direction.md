# Holding the direction

`rbg-taste` owns the direction ("Matchday, quiet luxury": Nike-clean chrome
× chunky pixel culture, one green that means "on the pitch, live, yours").
This file is Hallmark's half: how to keep a screen inside that direction
without sliding into what every model ships for "game UI", "esports",
"football" or "Nike-style". Load it when a screen drifts toward a cliché,
or before a new screen's first line of code.

## Before the first line of code

1. **Learn the screen's subject.** What only this screen has: its feature from the inventory, its job and hero in the intent map, its pixel art, the number the player cares about.
2. **Set aside the screen you would make anyway.** Picture what this brief gets without trying, and its obvious twist; neither ships. Yours is usually a heading top-left over a grid of bordered rounded boxes, a stat-tile wall, a rarity card grid, a glowing avatar on a podium, small grey tracked labels over every block. Two of those in one plan means the plan is the template.
3. **Go wide, then choose.** Sketch three compositions as far apart as the direction allows, a line each: what the hero is, where it sits, what the player does next. Build the one a design lead would fight for; borrow from the others only what makes it stronger.
4. **Let the hero run the screen.** The intent map names one hero per tab; composition, type size and the CTA all serve it. A screen whose hero is lost among boxes has no hero yet.

## Spent

What every model ships for this brief, refused on sight:

- **Generic game UI:** purple/navy night gradients, neon or orange glows, glowing borders on everything, gold "premium" accents, rarity rainbow frames, bevelled 3D buttons, slanted italic CTAs, shimmer sweeps, particle bursts on every reward.
- **Esports:** angled slashes and clipped corners on every container, hexagon frames, HUD brackets, "GG", "EPIC", "PRO", flames and lightning, carbon-fibre textures.
- **Football:** clip-art balls, trophies and shields drawn in SVG; grass textures and CSS-drawn pitches behind content; stadium-light flares; fake broadcast tickers; commentary-style copy.
- **"Nike-style" done badly:** a swoosh-like mark, `JUST` slogans, Futura copied, giant lifestyle photos the panel doesn't have, so minimal that progress, locked and live states vanish.
- **Pixel done badly:** pixel fonts on UI text, pixel borders or dithers on chrome, blurry upscaled sprites, scanlines as "retro" ambience.
- **Generic AI app:** glassmorphism, drop-shadowed floating panels, card in card, centred everything, small grey tracked labels over every head (54, 67), middle-dot meta strings (63), emoji as icons (30a).

## What a screen owes the direction

- One hero, one signal, the rest stepping back (`rbg-taste`).
- A composition its neighbouring tabs do not have (32), on the panel's shared grid.
- Something in the view big enough to be its reason (77).
- Copy that could only be RBG's (`craft.md` § 6).
- Every state designed: a list without `empty`, a buy without `locked` or `error`, is not done.
