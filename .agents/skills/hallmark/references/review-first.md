# First-screen review

You are reviewing the first screen of a game panel that is still being
built: Perfil's default state, the view every opening of the panel lands
on. You did not build it and will not be told why anything is the way it
is. Your inputs, and nothing else: its walkthrough frames at every host
size (`test-results/walkthrough/<project>/perfil--<sub>--default.png` for
`size-1024`, `size-1280`, `size-1600`, `shrunk`), the stamp's
first two lines, the Picks line's material and signature, the RBG badges in
`public/assets/brand/` if present, and the current app's Perfil screenshots
named in `docs/screen-inventory.md` for contrast. Batch your reads; stop
reading by the tenth turn and write.

First line: `ship` or `fix`.

Then at most five findings, one line each, worst first. Ask:

- Does the screen say who this player is in one second, in the fewest words, in a way only RBG could (a pixel footballer, a rank in PDL, a club)?
- Is the avatar the largest thing in the view, crisp (integer-scaled), on a plain field? Delete it in your head: if the screen still says the same thing, it is too weak or too small.
- Does anything read as a template or as the old app: a gradient or glow, a boxed stat wall, a label in tracked caps above a heading, a middle-dot meta line, emoji or Unicode icons, an orange/gold accent, a card in a card?
- Is there one signal, with green used only for something live, ready or the player's own?
- Does it hold at `shrunk`: no sideways scroll, nothing under the action bar, nothing squeezed into a sliver?
- Does the type carry the direction (athletic display figures, quiet body) at every size?

Never ask for copy, a fact or a picture the inventory does not have; say in
one line what the owner could supply. What `rbg-taste` prescribes is not a
finding. Do not fix anything.
